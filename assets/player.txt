# Player tuning. Times in seconds (60 Hz sim: 0.15 s = 9 frames).
hp 100
speed 5.5               # a notch above HL2's 190 u/s (4.83 m/s): Quake/Titanfall's base walk is brisk,
                         # not a stroll -- everything past this is Shift and technique, not the stick
sprint_mult 1.5455      # 5.5 * 1.5455 = 8.5 m/s, the speed Shift reaches in sprint_ramp -- and the
                         # hard ceiling on what holding W can ever ask for; see speed_cap below
turn_speed 16

# Ground movement (Half-Life/Quake shaped): walk 5.5 -> sprint 8.5 (speed * sprint_mult), reached in
# sprint_ramp seconds of holding Shift, full stop. For scale: Source sprint is 320 u/s = 8.13 m/s,
# Quake's sv_maxspeed 320 is the same number, Counter-Strike run is 250 u/s = 6.35 m/s, and Mirror's
# Edge tops Faith out around 8-9 m/s. accel/air_accel/friction are Quake's sv_accelerate/sv_friction-
# shaped coefficients, not m/s^2 -- see the friction/accel math in player_update (combat.c).
# jump_height is metres, converted to a launch vy against game.c's gravity. crouch_mult only
# scales speed here; the eye height and stance drop are game.c's job.
accel 12                # Source sv_accelerate 10, a touch higher: full wish speed inside 1/accel ~ 5 ticks
air_accel 6.0            # low on purpose: this is what makes air control a strafe-jump technique
                         # instead of a second accel step. See air_wish below -- the two multiply
                         # together into the m/s a clean strafe-jump earns per hop.
friction 7.5             # measured: releasing every key at 8.5 m/s stops in ~0.3 s (was Source's 4)
stop_speed 2.5           # Source sv_stopspeed 100 u/s = 2.54 m/s
jump_height 1.1          # -> vy ~8.4 m/s against game.c's 32 m/s^2 gravity, ~0.52 s airtime (measured)
crouch_mult 0.36         # 5.5 * 0.36 = 1.98 m/s, HL2 crouch 75 u/s = 1.9

# Traversal (src/combat.c, the "traversal" section). Sprint itself is no longer something you build:
# sprint_ramp seconds of holding Shift is essentially instant (a third of what it used to take) and
# that is the whole way to 8.5 -- the ground wishspeed a held Shift can ever ask for tops out there
# (top_speed in player_update), full stop. Above that is a momentum ceiling, speed_cap, that nothing
# but technique reaches: a chain of air-strafed hops, a slide jumped out of, a wall kicked off. Ground
# friction bleeds any of that back toward 8.5 the moment you stop chaining, which is what makes the
# ceiling a skill instead of a stat. sprint_surge/surge_ramp are the same latch stretched further,
# kept at 0 m/s now that sprint_ramp alone reaches the top -- the machinery (and the sprint_grace/
# sprint_decay gap-tolerance below) stays so a future slower sprint has somewhere to earn a long-run
# bonus without touching this file's shape. sprint_grace is how long the sprint survives a gap in the
# input (a stick reading zero for a tick while turning, a strafe-key swap, Shift lifting between
# presses) before it actually lets go -- that is what kills the stutter, none of those should read as
# letting go of Shift. Only a real stop spends sprint_decay seconds bleeding the built-up speed away.
# air_wish is the m/s the mid-air accel step chases and air_accel is deliberately low: their product
# is roughly the m/s per second a perfect strafe-jump earns, so a clean chain gains 0.3-0.6 m/s a hop
# (measured) instead of the old single air_accel doing the whole job and turning every jump into free
# speed. Both are dt-scaled in the accel step itself, so the gain is the same at 60 Hz and 144 Hz of
# real time -- the sim always ticks at a fixed rate regardless of the display (main.c's TICK_DT); a
# higher frame rate only samples the mouse more often between ticks, it does not add extra ticks.
sprint_ramp 0.15    # seconds from walk to the 8.5 m/s sprint (was 0.35, and 1.05 before that)
sprint_surge 0      # extra m/s a long sprint earns on top of 8.5 -- 0 now that sprint_ramp reaches
                     # the top on its own; see the block comment above
surge_ramp 2.5      # seconds of unbroken sprinting to earn all of sprint_surge (inert while it is 0)
sprint_grace 0.25   # seconds the sprint survives letting go of the stick / a turn
sprint_decay 0.6    # seconds for the built sprint to bleed away once it really stops
speed_cap 12.0      # the momentum ceiling: unreachable by holding W (top_speed above tops out at
                     # 8.5), only by technique -- air strafing, a slide jump, a wall jump
coyote 0.12        # seconds past the edge a jump still counts
jump_buffer 0.15   # seconds a jump press waits for the feet to land
air_wish 1.6       # m/s of wishspeed the air accel step chases; see air_accel above -- together they
                   # are what makes a strafe-jump earn 0.3-0.6 m/s instead of either flying or doing nothing

# Slide: Ctrl at a sprint. It bleeds slide_decel m/s every second and ends at slide_exit, or when
# the 0.85 s is up, or when Ctrl comes off -- unless there is a roof, in which case it holds until
# there is headroom, which is what makes a culvert something you go through rather than get stuck in.
# Jumping out keeps the slide's speed and adds SLIDE_JUMP_BOOST (combat.c) on top, capped by speed_cap.
slide_time 0.85
slide_decel 2.2
slide_enter 7.0    # only out of a real sprint now that a walk alone is 5.5 (was 4.6, then 6.0)
slide_exit 3.0
