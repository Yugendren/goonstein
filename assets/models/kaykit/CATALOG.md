# KayKit asset catalog

Full catalogue of every glTF model found in the four cloned KayKit packs
(bounding boxes computed from glTF accessor min/max on POSITION, transformed by
node translation/scale — rotation ignored, so sizes are approximate axis-aligned
bounds in the model's local/world space, in metres). `Materials` is the material
count found on the mesh. None of the KayKit materials in these packs use
`emissiveFactor`/`emissiveTexture`, and none are named with glow-related words —
light-source candidates below are identified by **file name** instead (torch,
candle, lantern, brazier, shrine).
