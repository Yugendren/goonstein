# island_intro.txt -- dusk, Pelican Pier. Four men have driven a rented motor boat three miles
# in the dark to rescue people they are not at all sure exist. ~32 s. Trigger: `intro`.
#
# The boat is a scene actor: island.txt tags it `name boat` and gives it a `collide ... deck` box,
# so `actor prop:boat move ...` drives it and the four men standing on that deck ride in with it.
# They step off onto the quay at the end; a `move` y is a floor, not an answer, so the 0.9 m from
# deck to quay is just the y in the line.
#
# Geography: the quay runs along X at y = 1.60, from x -81 to -57, its seaward edge at z = -115.7.
# The channel the boat comes up is z = -118.6, so the run in is a straight line along +X and the
# boat ends alongside, bow first, exactly where island.txt moors it.
