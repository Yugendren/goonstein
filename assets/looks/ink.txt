# SKETCH INK -- the drawn look DESIGN.md pillar 4 asks for.
#
# Full resolution. Lines come from the depth buffer (silhouettes) and from a step in luminance
# (interior creases: a folded arm, a window in a wall, the seam between two toon bands). The whole
# edge test is sampled from a point that wanders on an 8 Hz step, so the lines redraw themselves at
# a drawing's pace -- they boil, they do not shimmer. Shadow is crossed hatching rather than
# darkness. A tiling paper texture multiplies over the finished frame.
#
# The palette is deliberately two-note: everything is desaturated to about 45%, the highlights are
# gained warm and the shadows are lifted cool, so what survives is ochre light and blue shade. That
# is the accent pair; the level's own lamps and emissives are what break it.
#
# assets/textures/paper.png is generated by tools/make_paper.py -- if it is missing the paper layer
# is a silent no-op.
#
# `look include ink` in a level, or paste these lines.

look res      1.0
look ink      2 1.6 0.55     # 2 px lines, 1.6 px of boil at 8 Hz, a strong luminance edge
look hatch    0.55           # crossed rulings where the frame already went dark
look paper    0.85           # assets/textures/paper.png, multiplied
look flat     0.70           # mute the photoscans hard so the lines carry the image
look vignette 0.20

fogv     0.80 0.79 0.75   0.0040  0.0  0.020  0.15  50
                             # fog toward paper white: distance fades to the page
toon     0.05 0.66 2.0       # near-hard bands with a high floor -- a wash, not a shadow. A drawing
                             # has no black shadows; the hatching below is what darkness is made of.
grade    1.14 0.42 1.10 0.0 1.0
                             # bright, desaturated, low contrast: the hatching supplies the darks
lift     0.020 0.024 0.040   # cool shade
gain     1.06 1.00 0.92      # warm light -- the second accent
style    0 1.0 0 1           # the ink is the style layer's outline, driven by `look ink` above
pixel    0
shadow   0.55                # a soft sun shadow so the hatching has somewhere to live
