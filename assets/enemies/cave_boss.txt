# The thing at the bottom of the Culvert. Second boss, first one that is a fight rather than a duel.
#
# Everything here is read by boss_def_load (src/combat.c) and driven by src/boss.c -- NOT by the
# Warden's state machine, even though it shares the file format and the struct. The difference is
# the `kind` on each move: the Warden has four swings, this has four different KINDS of thing, and
# the fight is about which one is coming rather than about when to press parry. There is no parry.
#
# 600 hp against a pistol (34) and a rifle is about two minutes of sustained fire for one goon and
# well under a minute for four, which is the shape a co-op boss wants: long enough to have phases,
# short enough that being knocked down twice does not mean starting again.
name The Thing In The Culvert
model cave_boss              # assets/characters/cave_boss.txt (a placeholder; see that file)
hp 600
posture 100                  # unused: nothing here can be parried, so nothing spends posture
posture_regen 0
speed 3.4                    # walking. The charge is how it actually closes.
attack_range 6.0             # the distance it settles at between moves. Deliberately not melee
                             # range: a thing four metres tall standing on top of you fills the
                             # screen, and a telegraph drawn on the floor around its feet is the
                             # one thing you then cannot see. It closes to hit you and backs off.
think_min 0.35
think_max 0.90
phase2_hp 0.5                # below half it winds up faster and the volley comes twice
phase2_windup_mult 0.68
combo_chance 0.38

# --- stagger --- This boss has no guard to break, so damage breaks it instead: 120 points inside
# two seconds and it stops dead for a second and a half taking double. That is a deliberate ask --
# 120 is three and a half pistol magazines' worth of hits in two seconds, which one goon cannot do
# and three focusing the same window can. It is the co-op move.
stagger_damage 120
stagger_window 2.0
stagger_time 1.5
stagger_damage_mult 2.0

# --- damage_scale --- The one number that reconciles 600 hp with guns tuned against a goon's
# 100-point wind pool. A pistol round is 34 of that pool and a rifle round is 72; at face value one
# pistol empties this boss in four and a half seconds. At 0.30 a pistol round is 10 and a rifle round
# is 22, one goon does about 41 damage a second, and 600 hp is a fight of about a minute on your own
# -- less with mates, which is the direction a co-op boss should scale in. It also means the 120-in-
# two-seconds stagger above is genuinely a group move: one goon does 82 in two seconds and two do 164. The other
# way to stagger it is to bait a charge into a wall, which is the thing you can do alone.
damage_scale 0.30

aggro_range 0                # 0 = the whole cavern; there is nowhere in it to hide for long
retarget 2.5                 # seconds between reconsidering who it is angriest at

size 2.0 4.0 2.0             # a hit box you cannot miss, which is the point of a boss this size.
                             # assets/characters/cave_boss.txt's `scale` is set to match it: a model
                             # drawn bigger than its own collision cylinder puts the camera inside it.
color 0.42 0.30 0.34

# One move to a line (the parser reads a line at a time, so these do not wrap):
#   move NAME kind KIND windup S active S recovery S damage N range M parry no tell R G B
#        weight W cooldown S band MIN MAX p1 X p2 Y p3 Z step M clip NAME contact F
#
# The four tell colours are the fight's vocabulary, and they never swap:
#   red     you are standing in a line and it is about to run down it
#   violet  the floor is about to leave
#   orange  something is coming through the air
#   yellow  it is going to swing where it is standing

# (a) the charge. 0.9 s of windup, a red lane drawn on the floor, then 12 m/s for 16 m. Anything in
# the lane goes over. Running into a wall costs it most of a second on top of the recovery, which
# is the window the whole fight is built around: bait it into stone and empty a magazine.
move charge kind charge windup 0.90 active 1.6 recovery 1.0 damage 55 range 2.0 parry no tell 1.00 0.16 0.12 weight 1.3 cooldown 3.2 band 9 40 p1 12.0 p2 16.0 p3 1.7 clip 2H_Melee_Attack_Chop contact 0.5

# (b) the slam. 1.2 s, a violet ring on the floor at 6 m, then a shockwave half a metre high coming
# out of the ground at 11 m/s. Half a metre is under the 1.1 m jump in assets/player.txt, so the
# answer is in the air and nowhere else -- not behind a pillar, not further away.
# Its band stops at 9 m and the charge's starts at 9: a ring six metres across thrown from twelve
# metres away is a move nobody has to answer, and a boss whose moves do not reach is not scary,
# it is a fireworks display. Inside nine metres this is the likeliest thing it does.
move slam kind slam windup 1.20 active 0.45 recovery 1.1 damage 45 range 6.0 parry no tell 0.72 0.32 1.00 weight 1.6 cooldown 3.0 band 0 9 p1 6.0 p2 11.0 p3 0.5 clip 2H_Melee_Attack_Chop contact 0.55

# (c) the volley. Five slow fireballs at whoever it is angry at, one every fifth of a second, each
# aimed where they are and not where they will be. Ten in phase two. Walking sideways beats it;
# standing behind a pillar beats it completely, which is what the pillars are for.
move volley kind volley windup 0.70 active 1.4 recovery 0.9 damage 22 range 40 parry no tell 1.00 0.62 0.15 weight 1.1 cooldown 2.8 band 5 40 p1 5 p2 0.20 clip 2H_Melee_Attack_Slice contact 0.4

# (d) the sweep. What it does when you are close enough to be hit by an arm. Short, cheap, and the
# reason standing next to it is a decision rather than a default.
move sweep kind sweep windup 0.55 active 0.22 recovery 0.65 damage 38 range 4.2 parry no tell 1.00 0.86 0.25 weight 1.2 cooldown 1.2 band 0 6.5 step 2.2 clip 2H_Melee_Attack_Slice contact 0.42
