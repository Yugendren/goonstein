# island.txt -- GOONSTEIN ISLAND, the M3 skeleton. Plain polygon look, dusk, third person.
#
# Everything here is fictional. The outline and the scale are a caricature of a public
# satellite shape (see ISLAND.md); every name, building and joke is invented.
#
# AXES, once and for all:
#     world +Z = island EAST      world -Z = island WEST
#     world -X = island NORTH     world +X = island SOUTH
# so the long axis runs along Z: the boat lands at the north-west, the compound sits on the
# western shelf, and the striped pavilion stands on the eastern high point 300 m away.
#
#     z -152 .. -122   Slack Tide Cove, the west beach                 (trigger cove)
#     z -118 .. -106   Pelican Pier, the stone mole and the boat       (trigger dock, intro)
#     z -113 .. -105   the Culvert mouth, cut into the bluff behind it (trigger door:cave)
#     z -100 ..  -84   Pad One, the helipad on the bluff               (trigger helipad)
#     z  -84 ..  -56   Villa Ambergris and its courtyard               (trigger compound)
#     z  -64 ..  -32   The Oval, the pool house and Cabana Row         (trigger pool)
#     z  -45 ..  -20   The Bunkhouse, staff quarters                   (trigger bunkhouse)
#     z  -14 ..    6   the saddle and the Cistern water tower          (trigger cistern)
#     z   34 ..   58   Windward Point, the cliff lookout               (trigger lookout)
#     z   78 ..  100   Utility Two and the Culvert cutting, the back way (trigger culvert, tunnel)
#     z  102 ..  124   the Court, a bermed rectangle nobody explains   (trigger court)
#     z  112 ..  140   the Music Room, the striped pavilion            (trigger temple)
#
# Terrain, biome colours and the vegetation scatter at the bottom of this file are generated by
# tools/island_terrain.py; y values were snapped to the ground by tools/island_snap.py.
# See ISLAND_BUILD.md.

# service cutting behind Utility Two, three hundred metres up the island, and it still works -- it

# ================================================================ THE WALK TO THE CULVERT
# `route NAME x z ...` is a walkable line through the level, written down (see assets/levels/
# README.md): not navigation, not a path-finder, just the answer to "which way round does a person
# actually go", kept in the file that knows. It is named after the trigger it leads to, which is how
# src/bossbot.c finds it.
#
# It used to be 269 metres long, because the only way into the Culvert was the service cutting
# behind Utility Two at the far end of the island, and the straight line to it climbs the hill and
# puts you on TOP of the cutting's roof slab, two metres over the doorway and outside the trigger.
# That walk was the bug: nobody could find the cave. The cave now opens in the bluff behind the mole
# (see THE CULVERT MOUTH above) and the route is four points and about fourteen metres of beach
# gravel. The old door still works -- it is the same `door:cave` -- but it is the back way in now,
# and the back way in does not need a line written down for it: a route leads to the door you are
# meant to find, and the objective (src/map.h) points at whichever `door:` trigger is nearer.
#
