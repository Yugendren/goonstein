# Third-party asset manifest

Every asset not authored in this repo is listed here with its source and licence.
Allowed licences: CC0, OFL, MIT, Mixamo. Not allowed: any CC-BY-NC, any ShareAlike, anything
requiring attribution we cannot satisfy in-game.

Poly Haven assets below (`assets/models/polyhaven/`, `assets/textures/`) are CC0 1.0
(https://polyhaven.com/license), fetched with `tools/polyhaven_get.py`; none of it is
AI-generated — every model is a photoscan and every texture is a photographed/scanned surface map.
